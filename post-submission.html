STEP-BY-STEP RECRUITMENT LIFECYCLE

The 7 Stages from Application to Gulf Deployment

Clear expectations and estimated timelines for each stage so you never have to wonder about your application status.

1

Application Submitted & Token Allocated

Immediate / Day 1

Your profile is registered in the AVC Candidate Database CRM. A unique application reference token (e.g. AVC-APP-2026-XXXX) is generated and sent to you via instant WhatsApp auto-reply and on-screen confirmation.

📌 What you need to do: Note down your Application Token and ensure your WhatsApp number remains active.
2

Profile & Document Verification

Estimated: 1–2 Days

Our technical recruitment desk reviews your declared trade experience, technical certificates (ITI / Diploma), and passport validity. Passport must have at least 8 months remaining validity.

📌 What you need to do: Keep clear scanned photos of your Passport (front & back) and trade experience letters ready on your phone.
3

Requirement Matching & Visa Block Allocation

Estimated: 3–5 Days

When an overseas employer or licensed Recruiting Agent issues a demand for your trade (e.g., 50 Electricians for Saudi Arabia, 30 Welders for UAE), our CRM algorithm matches your experience and passport status (ECR/ECNR) to the vacancy criteria.

📌 What you need to do: Check the official AVC Vacancy Board and keep an eye on our WhatsApp Channel alerts.
4

Interview & Practical Trade Test Invitation

Estimated: 5–7 Days

Shortlisted candidates receive an official call-up notice on WhatsApp with the interview date, reporting time, and testing instructions at the Assignment Venue Center campus in Darbhanga, Bihar.

📌 What you need to do: Confirm your attendance within 24 hours. Prepare your safety gear (safety shoes, work clothes) for hands-on practical testing.
5

Hands-On Trade Testing & Client Evaluation

Day of Interview

You execute a 4-hour practical evaluation in our specialized testing bays (Lincoln 6G welding booths, 415V electrical benches, or trailer obstacle yard) witnessed in-person by visiting client delegates or recorded in HD video for foreign technical panels.

📌 What you need to do: Read our Technical Trade Test & Interview Guide to review common circuits, weld joints, and troubleshooting questions beforehand.
6

Employer Selection & Offer Letter Briefing

Estimated: 2–4 Days Post-Test

Selected candidates are briefed on the official Job Offer: Basic Salary, Food Allowance, Working Hours, Overtime terms, Free Accommodation, and Medical Insurance. You receive a copy of your provisional selection confirmation.

📌 Statutory Rule: Verify that the salary matches the approved MEA eMigrate minimum wage standards before signing.
7

GAMCA Medical, PGE Clearance & Flight Mobilization

Estimated: 14–25 Days

Candidate completes the mandatory GAMCA medical fitness test at an authorized clinic. Selected files are forwarded to the authorized Recruiting Agent for visa endorsement, MEA eMigrate clearance (for ECR passports), and ticket issuance.

✈️ Deployment: Final pre-departure orientation briefing (PDOT) and flight departure to the destination country.
