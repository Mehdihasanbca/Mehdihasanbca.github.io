Transparency / who, how and why

AVC publishes recruitment guidance to help people make better decisions before they apply, pay or travel.

This policy explains how our public guides are prepared, reviewed, sourced, corrected and kept separate from vacancy advertising or commercial pressure.

Last reviewed

20 August 2026. When a material policy changes, this page and affected articles should be updated together.

01 / OWNERSHIP

Who is responsible for the content?

Publisher

Assignment Venue Center (AVC), a recruitment-support and manpower coordination business based in Darbhanga, Bihar, publishes the guides and knowledge pages on this website.

Editorial review

Mehdi Hasan, Founder & Business Head, reviews the editorial direction, operational accuracy and consistency with AVC's published recruitment-support role. This does not create or claim a professional licence, government authority or legal qualification that AVC does not hold.

02 / SOURCE STANDARD

How we decide what can be stated as fact.

Operational guidance

We use recurring candidate, employer and recruitment-partner questions from AVC's recruitment-support work to decide which topics deserve a guide. Examples include profile preparation, interview readiness, document handling, requirement writing and fraud verification.

Rules and official processes

When a page discusses Indian overseas-employment rules, Recruiting Agents, emigration procedures or Wafid processes, we prefer primary sources such as the Ministry of External Affairs, eMigrate, Wafid/Gulf Health Council and other official government or program websites.

What we do not treat as proof

Forwarded WhatsApp messages, agency posters, social-media claims and screenshots may help identify a question, but they are not used as final authority for legal, fee or government-process claims.

Vacancy-specific facts

Salary, duty hours, benefits, employer identity, interview method and selection criteria can change by demand. Guides explain how to verify these facts; they do not convert an old vacancy into a permanent rule.

03 / ACCURACY

Clarity matters more than sounding certain.

No guaranteed outcomes

We do not state that registration, interview, medical fitness, payment or profile submission guarantees selection, visa approval or overseas deployment.

No fabricated experience

AVC guidance does not recommend fake experience letters, altered certificates, invented project histories or false trade claims. Profile support should improve presentation of genuine information.

Role boundaries

AVC is presented as a sourcing, recruitment-support and coordination business. Where Indian law requires a registered Recruiting Agent or an authorized foreign-employer process, we identify that distinction rather than implying AVC holds that regulated role.

Dates and changing information

Each long-form guide shows an update date. Time-sensitive figures such as official fees should be attributed to the source and accompanied by a reminder to check the current official page.

04 / CORRECTIONS

If a fact changes or we get something wrong.

Material corrections

When a factual error changes the meaning of guidance—such as a fee, official procedure or responsibility—we update the article and its modified date. We do not silently preserve outdated advice for search traffic.

Reader corrections

Readers can report a suspected factual error through AVC's official contact route or by email at info@assignmentvenuecentre.me. Include the page URL and the source that supports the correction.

05 / COMMERCIAL SEPARATION

Helpful information should still be useful if the reader never buys a service.

Guides vs vacancies

Editorial guides are designed to remain useful beyond a single hiring demand. Job posts belong in the Jobs Hub and should not be disguised as evergreen advice.

Guides vs AVC services

A guide may link to an AVC tool or service when relevant, but the article should still explain the process well enough for a reader to act independently where the official route allows it.

Ads

If advertising is enabled on the site, advertising does not determine what an AVC guide says. We do not write a conclusion to satisfy an advertiser, and we do not present ads as AVC recommendations.

Payments

AVC service fees should be separated from official government, Wafid, medical-centre or other third-party fees. Payment trust and refund information is published separately from editorial articles.

06 / PRIVACY & SAFETY

We avoid publishing unnecessary personal data in editorial content.

Candidate examples

Guides use generalized examples instead of publishing candidate passport numbers, medical records, phone numbers or private case details.

Sensitive subjects

Medical, payment and identity-document guidance focuses on process safety and official verification. Individual medical fitness or legal outcomes are not diagnosed or guaranteed through editorial content.
