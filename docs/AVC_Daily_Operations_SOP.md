## 2. Inbound Lead Handling Protocol (Within 60 Minutes)

1. **Verify Trade Category**:
   Match candidate's declared trade against active Gulf demands:
   * **High Priority**: 6G TIG/ARC Welders, Industrial Electricians, Chiller Technicians, Heavy Trailer Drivers.
   * **General**: Civil Masons, Plumbers, Steel Fixers, Duct Erectors.
2. **Passport Check (ECR vs ECNR)**:
   * **ECNR**: Direct client interview and visa stamping.
   * **ECR**: Ensure registered eMigrate employer route is mapped.
   * **Validity Check**: If expiry < 8 months, immediately advise candidate to apply for renewal at nearest Darbhanga/Patna Passport Seva Kendra.
3. **WhatsApp Auto-Dispatch**:
   Use pre-approved template from `docs/AVC_WhatsApp_Recruitment_Automation_Flow.md`. Ensure Application Token is included.

## 3. Physical Trade Testing SOP (Darbhanga Campus)

1. **Morning Candidate Check-In**:
   * Inspect original passport and Aadhaar card (identity fraud check).
   * Confirm candidate has appropriate safety shoes and work gear.
   * Issue printed Hall Ticket / Evaluation Sheet ([`candidate-scorecard.html`](file:///c:/Users/MehdiHasanBCA-2023/Desktop/Assighnment/candidate-scorecard.html)).
2. **Bay Allocation & Tool Setup**:
   * **Electricians**: Bench #01 to #04 (415V Star-Delta contactors, timers, Megger tester).
   * **Welders**: Booth #01 to #12 (Lincoln multi-process machine, Argon gas cylinder, 2" Sch 40 pipe coupon).
   * **HVAC**: Diagnostics bay (manifold gauges, vacuum pump, compressor terminal wiring).
3. **Dual-Camera Recording**:
   * Camera 1: Wide angle capturing candidate face and physical stance.
   * Camera 2: Close-up macro lens zoomed onto weld puddle or wire ferrules.
4. **Grading & Scoring**:
   * Chief Examiner inspects coupon, conducts dye-penetrant (DP) test or Megger insulation reading.
   * Examiner marks scorecard out of 100 and signs official result.
