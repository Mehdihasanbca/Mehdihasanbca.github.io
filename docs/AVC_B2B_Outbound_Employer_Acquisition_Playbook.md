## 1. Executive Summary & Value Proposition

Gulf general contractors, MEP subcontractors, and facility management (FM) firms face severe operational bottlenecks:
1. **High Trade Test Failure Rates (30–45%)**: Traditional recruiting agents send unvetted CVs; when foreign interviewers arrive in India, half the candidates cannot read an electrical schematic or make a sound 6G weld root pass.
2. **Delayed Mobilization**: Unorganized document screening and delayed GAMCA medicals push project handover dates.
3. **Regulatory & Compliance Anxiety**: Fear of dealing with unlicensed intermediaries or extortionate candidate fee racketeering that invites MEA/embassy audits.

### The AVC Differentiator (Pitch Anchor)
* **Pre-Tested Before Client Arrival**: Candidates undergo a rigorous 4-hour practical test at our Darbhanga facility (Lincoln 6G welding bays, 415V electrical benches, chiller troubleshooting).
* **HD Video Testing Dossiers**: Clients receive uncut 1080p video recordings of welding coupons, Megger test readings, or vehicle maneuvering before booking flight tickets.
* **Strict Emigration Act 1983 & ₹0 Candidate Fee Policy**: Zero commercial intake from candidates ensures maximum worker morale, zero flight risks, and full statutory compliance.
* **250 Candidates/Day Testing & Interview Capacity**: 4 air-conditioned client interview suites, biometric candidate logging, and high-speed fiber connectivity for live remote interviews.
