03

04

02 / SAFETY

Use the application route with the right expectations.

A

No selection guarantee

Registration does not guarantee shortlisting, selection, visa approval or deployment.

B

Use factual information

Do not submit false experience, trade qualifications, documents or work-sample claims.

C

Verify recruitment stakeholders

Employer and registered Recruiting Agent details should be verified where they are part of the formal overseas recruitment process.

D

Use official AVC channels

Return to the AVC website, current Jobs board or Trust Center when you need to verify a link or requirement.

03 / HANDOFF

What AVC handles, and what happens after.

Recruitment boundary: AVC provides recruitment support and coordination. Final selection, recruitment approval, visa processing, emigration and overseas deployment are handled by the concerned employer and/or registered Recruiting Agent, subject to the applicable process and verification.
