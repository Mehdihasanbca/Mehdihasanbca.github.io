Interview coordination

Interview logistics should be clear before the candidate arrives.

AVC coordinates shortlist communication, schedules, attendance and candidate readiness for online or physical recruitment interviews.

01 / SCOPE

What moves through this service.

AVC coordinates

  • Shortlist communication
  • Date, time and venue / meeting link
  • Candidate attendance confirmation
  • Role-specific readiness instructions
  • Interview-day queue or registration support when applicable
  • Confirmed result follow-up

Candidate prepares

  • Join or arrive on time
  • Keep requested documents ready
  • Present actual experience clearly
  • Follow the role-specific interview instruction
  • Treat interview invitation as a step, not final selection
02 / FLOW

Operating sequence.

01

Shortlist

Eligible candidates are identified for interview.

02

Schedule

Time, venue or online link is communicated.

03

Attend

Candidates join or report as instructed.

04

Outcome

Confirmed results move into the next recruitment step.

Decision point

Coordination and selection are separate roles.

AVC coordinates interviews and communication. The concerned employer or authorized recruitment stakeholder makes the final selection decision.
