Service 05

Interview coordination

AVC supports online and physical interview scheduling, candidate communication, attendance coordination and interview-day organization.

Formats: employer video interview, online interview support and physical interview coordination.

Coordination support

  • Candidate shortlist communication
  • Date, time and venue / meeting-link coordination
  • Candidate attendance confirmation
  • Basic interview-readiness instructions
  • Interview-day registration and queue support where applicable
  • Selection-result follow-up when confirmed by the concerned stakeholder

For candidates

  • Arrive or join on time
  • Keep requested documents ready
  • Present actual experience truthfully
  • Follow role-specific interview instructions
  • Do not treat an interview invitation as final selection
