01 / INTERVIEW BRIEF

A candidate should be able to confirm the interview brief before attending.

ROLE

What position?

Know the actual role or trade being assessed and the relevant vacancy context.

WHEN

What date and time?

Use the confirmed schedule and account for reporting or login time when instructed.

WHERE

Physical or online?

Confirm the venue, reporting point or meeting platform/link from a known source.

WHAT TO BRING

Which items?

Keep only the requested CV, documents or work evidence ready for that interview.

WHO

Who is coordinating?

Know whether communication is through AVC, the employer or the applicable recruitment stakeholder.

NEXT STEP

How will results move?

Wait for confirmed outcome communication rather than treating attendance as selection.

02 / INTERVIEW MODE

Online and physical interviews need different operational preparation.

ONLINE / REMOTE

Technical readiness

  • Charge the phone or device and keep power backup where possible
  • Test internet, camera, microphone and meeting application
  • Use a quiet, well-lit space and a stable camera position
  • Keep the confirmed meeting link private and ready
  • Join using the candidate identity expected by the interview coordinator
PHYSICAL / VENUE

Reporting readiness

  • Confirm the exact venue and reporting time before travel
  • Carry requested documents in an organized form
  • Allow enough travel time to avoid late arrival
  • Follow venue registration, queue and safety instructions
  • Do not hand original documents to an unknown person without understanding the process
03 / CANDIDATE PREP

Prepare to explain the same real experience shown in the profile.

INTRODUCTION

Name, current location, trade/position and concise relevant experience.

Keep the introduction consistent with the submitted profile.

WORK HISTORY

Be ready to explain recent employer/project, period and actual responsibilities.

Do not memorize experience that cannot be demonstrated or explained.

TRADE DETAIL

Explain real tools, machines, systems, tasks or work environments relevant to the role.

Specific genuine examples are stronger than broad unsupported claims.

ROLE TERMS

Understand the vacancy information that has been officially communicated.

Ask for clarification when important terms are unclear rather than assuming.

COMMUNICATION

Answer clearly, briefly and truthfully; ask for repetition if a question is not understood.

Language fluency should not be exaggerated in the profile or interview.

04 / OPERATING FLOW

AVC coordination moves through four observable steps.

01 / SHORTLIST

Candidate identified

An applicable recruitment process identifies candidates for interview.

02 / SCHEDULE

Brief confirmed

Date, time, mode, venue/link and preparation instructions are communicated when available.

03 / ATTEND

Interview happens

The candidate joins or reports as instructed and presents actual role-relevant experience.

04 / OUTCOME

Confirmed result

Only confirmed results should move a candidate into the applicable next recruitment step.

DECISION RULE

AVC coordinates interview communication; it does not replace the employer or authorized recruitment stakeholder as the final selection decision-maker.

05 / AFTER INTERVIEW

Wait for a confirmed outcome before treating the process as selected.

AVC may coordinate confirmed result communication and applicable next steps. Final selection and further overseas recruitment, visa/emigration and deployment processing remain with the concerned employer and/or registered Recruiting Agent.
