Working model

Clear coordination from requirement to authorized processing.

Page 04

Requirement

A written manpower requirement is received through an employer, registered RA or recruitment partner.

Sourcing

AVC communicates the requirement and collects suitable candidate applications.

Screening

Candidate profiles are organized against available role criteria.

Interview

Eligible candidates are coordinated for the agreed interview process.

Processing

Selected candidates continue through the responsible employer/RA formalities.

Recruitment responsibility: AVC provides recruitment support and coordination. Final selection, recruitment approval, visa processing, emigration and overseas deployment are handled by the concerned employer and/or registered Recruiting Agent, subject to applicable law and verification.
Candidates

Apply & prepare

  • Register through the official AVC application route.
  • Keep accurate CV, passport and role information ready.
  • Follow official interview instructions.
  • Verify vacancy and recruitment stakeholders.
Employers

Share a clear manpower brief

  • Company/project and work location.
  • Job title, quantity and experience criteria.
  • Salary, duty hours and benefits.
  • Interview and recruitment route.
Recruitment partners

Work through documented channels

  • Legal agency identity and authorization where applicable.
  • Written manpower demand and employer/project details.
  • Selection and processing responsibilities.
  • Documented commercial and recruitment terms.
