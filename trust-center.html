Recruitment boundary

What AVC does — and what AVC does not claim to do.

AVC can support

  • Candidate sourcing and application intake
  • CV/profile preparation guidance
  • Interview coordination
  • Document-readiness guidance
  • Employer and recruitment-partner communication
  • Requirement-based screening coordination

Do not treat AVC support as

  • A guarantee of selection, visa or deployment
  • Embassy or government authorization without specific evidence
  • Independent overseas Recruiting Agent authority where a separate licence is legally required
  • Trade-test or skill-certification authority unless separately authorized

Overseas recruitment: final selection, recruitment approval, visa processing, emigration and overseas deployment are handled by the concerned employer and/or registered Recruiting Agent, subject to applicable law and verification.

Before you proceed

AVC verification checklist.

1. Check the domain

Use assignmentvenuecentre.me and links published from this website.

2. Check the vacancy

Confirm role, country, employer/RA reference, salary/benefits and current status before acting.

3. Check communication

Be cautious of unknown personal accounts, copied logos or pressure tactics.

4. Check documents

Share sensitive documents only when required for a verified recruitment process.

5. Check payments

Ask for written terms, purpose of payment and proper receipt before making a legitimate payment.

6. Keep records

Retain job posts, messages, receipts, agreements and recruitment references.
