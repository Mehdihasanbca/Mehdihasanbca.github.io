03 / RESPONSIBILITY MAP

Trust improves when each party's role is clear.

AVC recruitment support can include

  • Candidate sourcing and application coordination
  • CV/profile and document readiness guidance
  • Interview scheduling and candidate communication
  • Employer and recruitment-partner coordination
  • Requirement-based screening support

Employer / registered Recruiting Agent process handles

  • Final hiring selection and recruitment approval
  • Formal overseas recruitment authorization where applicable
  • Visa and emigration processing where applicable
  • Employment documentation, travel and deployment
  • Government or embassy procedures requiring authorized action
SELECTION RULE

Registration with AVC, a candidate profile, interview attendance or document submission does not by itself guarantee selection, visa approval or overseas deployment.
