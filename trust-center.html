Recruitment roles

AVC support and overseas recruitment responsibilities.

Knowing which party handles each step makes the process easier to verify.

AVC can coordinate

  • Candidate sourcing and application intake
  • CV/profile readiness
  • Interview coordination
  • Document checklist guidance
  • Employer and recruitment-partner communication
  • Requirement-based screening support

Employer / Recruiting Agent process handles

  • Final selection and recruitment approval
  • Visa and emigration processing where applicable
  • Formal employment documentation
  • Travel and overseas deployment
  • Government or embassy procedures requiring specific authorization

Selection: AVC registration or support does not guarantee a job, visa approval or overseas deployment.
