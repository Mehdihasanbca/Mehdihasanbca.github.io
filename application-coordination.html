Service 02

Application coordination

AVC helps keep candidate applications organized from registration through vacancy communication, screening follow-up and interview readiness.

Official application path: use the AVC registration form linked from this website.

What AVC coordinates

  • Candidate registration and profile collection
  • Vacancy-specific application communication
  • Basic completeness checks on supplied information
  • Follow-up for screening, interview or requested updates
  • Status communication where AVC receives confirmed information

Candidate responsibility

  • Submit accurate current details
  • Use reachable contact information
  • Keep CV and relevant documents ready
  • Respond to verified requests on time
  • Do not treat registration as a selection guarantee
Candidate pathway

A clearer application journey.

1

Register

Candidate submits the official AVC form.

2

Review

Profile is considered against suitable verified requirements.

3

Coordinate

AVC communicates next-step information when applicable.

4

Proceed

Selected candidates continue through the concerned employer or recruitment partner process.

AVC coordinates applications and follow-up. Final selection and further processing depend on the concerned employer and authorized recruitment stakeholders.
