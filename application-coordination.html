01 / APPLICATION RECORD

Three records should remain aligned through the application.

The vacancy, the candidate and the next action should not become disconnected messages.

VACANCY CONTEXT

Know what you are applying for.

Use the vacancy reference, role, location and published requirements when a specific AVC vacancy is involved.

CANDIDATE RECORD

Use current factual information.

Name, contact details, role, experience and other profile information should match the candidate's real background.

NEXT ACTION

Follow only confirmed instructions.

Interview, document or other follow-up steps should come from the applicable recruitment process—not assumptions after form submission.

02 / QUALITY CHECK

A complete application is readable, reachable and role-relevant.

Contact

Current mobile/WhatsApp and email that the candidate actively checks.

Wrong or unreachable contact details can break follow-up.

Role

The trade or position the candidate can genuinely perform.

Do not select unrelated roles only to increase application count.

Experience

Actual work history and role-relevant responsibilities.

Do not increase dates, titles or qualifications to match a vacancy.

Reference

Use the AVC vacancy reference when the application came from a published vacancy.

General registration is separate from claiming a specific vacancy match.

Files

Prepare only the documents or media relevant to the actual stage.

Sensitive records should not be broadcast through unverified channels.

03 / OPERATING FLOW

Application coordination follows a simple handoff sequence.

01 / REGISTER

Application

Candidate uses the official AVC application route with current factual details.

02 / REVIEW

Context check

Available profile information can be reviewed against an applicable requirement.

03 / COORDINATE

Next step

AVC communicates applicable document, screening or interview instructions when available.

04 / HANDOFF

Responsible stakeholder

Selected candidates proceed with the concerned employer and/or registered Recruiting Agent for applicable further processing.

04 / CANDIDATE CONTROL

Keep enough information to verify your own application trail.

Keep with you

  • The vacancy reference when one exists
  • A copy of the CV/profile version you submitted
  • Important written instructions received through known channels
  • Interview details and document requests relevant to the process

Do not assume

  • A form submission means selection
  • An interview invitation means final approval
  • Every candidate needs the same documents or later-stage formalities
  • An unknown message is official only because it mentions AVC or a vacancy
SAFETY RULE

When a request looks unusual, check the vacancy, sender and official AVC route before sharing sensitive documents or acting on payment instructions.

05 / HANDOFF

Application support ends where formal selection and authorized processing begin.

AVC supports registration, candidate coordination and communication. Final selection and applicable overseas recruitment, visa/emigration and deployment steps remain with the concerned employer and/or registered Recruiting Agent.
