Before sourcing starts

The information AVC expects from a recruitment partner.

A clear requirement protects both the candidate experience and the business relationship.

Requirement information

  • Legal business / agency identity
  • Recruitment licence or authorization details where applicable
  • Written manpower demand / job requirement
  • Employer and project information appropriate to the process
  • Salary, benefits, duty hours and contract terms
  • Interview and selection procedure
  • Responsibility for visa, travel and deployment

How AVC prefers to work

  • Job and employer details should come from the actual requirement
  • Selection and deployment expectations should be communicated accurately
  • Agreed recruitment responsibilities should be respected
  • Candidate data should be used for the agreed recruitment purpose
  • Commercial terms should be documented before significant sourcing work
  • Recruitment activity should follow the applicable legal route
Working flow

A practical partner-to-AVC recruitment process.

Confirm

Confirm partner identity, role and the manpower requirement.

Brief

Agree job details, sourcing criteria, commercial terms and interview route.

Source

AVC coordinates candidate outreach and application collection.

Select

Profiles and interviews move through the agreed selection process.

Process

Selected candidates continue through the responsible employer / Recruiting Agent formalities and deployment route.

Recruitment responsibility

Licensed recruitment responsibilities stay with the authorized party.

Where overseas recruitment law requires a licensed Recruiting Agent, AVC remains the sourcing and recruitment-support partner. The licensed RA and/or authorized employer handles the regulated recruitment, visa, emigration and deployment process.
