Partnership standards

AVC expects clarity before candidate mobilization.

Partner information expected

  • Legal business / agency identity
  • Recruitment licence or authorization details where legally applicable
  • Written manpower demand / job requirement
  • Employer and project information appropriate to the process
  • Salary, benefits, duty hours and contract terms
  • Interview and selection procedure
  • Clear responsibility for visa, emigration, travel and deployment

AVC working principles

  • No fabricated demand, employer identity or visa claim
  • No guaranteed selection or deployment statement
  • No bypass of clearly documented recruitment responsibility
  • Candidate and business information should be handled for the agreed recruitment purpose
  • Commercial terms should be documented before significant sourcing or deployment work
  • Recruitment activity must follow applicable law and authorized channels

Public compliance position

AVC does not replace a legally required Recruiting Agent.

Where overseas recruitment law requires a registered/licensed Recruiting Agent, AVC's role remains sourcing, recruitment support and coordination. The registered RA and/or authorized employer remains responsible for the regulated recruitment, visa, emigration and deployment process.
