02 / BEFORE SOURCING

The information AVC expects from a recruitment partner.

A clear demand protects the candidate experience and the business relationship.

Partner identity

Legal business / agency name and authorized contact.

Licence or authorization details should be supplied where applicable.

Demand

Written manpower requirement with positions, quantity, criteria and project context.

Job details should come from the actual requirement.

Terms

Salary, benefits, duty hours, contract terms and material candidate conditions.

Selection and deployment expectations should be communicated accurately.

Selection

Interview, assessment, shortlist and result communication procedure.

Candidate data should be used only for the agreed recruitment purpose.

Responsibility

Who handles visa, emigration, travel, deployment and other regulated formalities.

Recruitment activity should follow the applicable legal route.

Commercial

Coordination terms and responsibilities agreed before significant sourcing work.

Written terms reduce bypass, misunderstanding and candidate confusion.

04 / RECRUITMENT BOUNDARY

Licensed recruitment responsibilities stay with the authorized party.

AUTHORIZED ROLE

Where overseas recruitment law requires a licensed/registered Recruiting Agent, AVC remains the sourcing and recruitment-support partner. The concerned RA and/or authorized employer handles regulated recruitment, visa, emigration and deployment processing.
