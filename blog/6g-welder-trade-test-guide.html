What is the 6G Welding Position?

In high-pressure piping, refinery construction, and offshore drilling platforms, pipe joints cannot be rotated during fabrication. The pipe must be welded in whatever angle or orientation the engineering layout dictates.

The 6G position under ASME Section IX (or AWS D1.1 / EN ISO 9606-1) places a test pipe coupon inclined at a fixed 45-degree angle (±5°) without rotation. Because the welder must execute welds across flat, vertical, overhead, and horizontal positions in a single continuous joint, qualifying in 6G certifies a tradesman for virtually all welding positions (1G, 2G, 3G, 4G, 5G, and 6G).

Step 1: Fit-Up and Bevel Preparation

A flawless weld begins with mechanical joint preparation. Examiners at testing venues like Assignment Venue Center inspect the test piece before the arc is ever struck:

  • Bevel Angle: Standard 37.5-degree bevel (total 75-degree included V-groove angle).
  • Root Face (“Land”): 1.5mm to 2.5mm uniform root face ground smoothly along the circumference without knife edges.
  • Root Gap: 2.5mm to 3.2mm uniform root gap maintained with a spacer rod or TIG filler wire.
  • Tack Welds: Typically 4 small bridge tacks (or feather-edge tacks) at 12, 3, 6, and 9 o’clock positions using GTAW with complete fusion.
  • Cleaning: Bright metal grinding to at least 25mm back from the bevel inside and out to remove mill scale, rust, and moisture.

Step 2: TIG (GTAW) Root Pass Mastery

For cross-country oil pipelines and refinery process lines, the root run is virtually always performed using Gas Tungsten Arc Welding (GTAW / TIG) with 99.99% pure Argon shielding gas (and an Argon backing purge for stainless or chrome alloys):

Parameters: 2.4mm 2% Thoriated or Lanthanated Tungsten electrode, ER70S-6 filler wire (2.0mm or 2.4mm), amperage set between 85A and 110A DCEN (Direct Current Electrode Negative).

Key Examiner Checks: Full root penetration with a smooth, concave-to-flush internal bead reinforcement (0.5mm to 1.5mm). Lack of penetration (LOP), root concavity (“suck-back”), or burn-through causes immediate coupon rejection.

Step 3: Hot Pass, Fill & Cap using SMAW (E7018)

Once the root pass is approved by the welding inspector, the remaining passes are deposited using Shielded Metal Arc Welding (SMAW / Stick) or continued GTAW:

  1. Hot Pass: Deposited quickly after root cleaning using E7018 low-hydrogen electrodes (or E6010 for downhill cellulosic pipelines) to burn out minor inclusions and build bead thickness.
  2. Fill Passes: Multi-pass stringer beads or slight weaves deposited with complete inter-pass slag removal using chipping hammer and wire wheel. Interpass temperatures must be monitored (typically below 250°C).
  3. Capping Pass: The visible cosmetic crown. Width must not extend more than 2.5mm beyond the bevel edge on either side; cap height should be uniform between 1.0mm and 2.5mm with smooth wash-in to the pipe wall without undercut.

Visual and Radiographic Testing (RT / X-Ray)

After cooling, the coupon undergoes strict non-destructive examination (NDE):

  • Visual Inspection (VT): Zero cracks, zero lack of fusion, undercut depth strictly ≤ 0.5mm, zero excessive spatter, and complete arc strike removal.
  • Radiographic Testing (RT / X-Ray): An industrial gamma source (Iridium-192 or X-Ray tube) produces radiographic films of the entire joint. The film is evaluated against ASME B31.3 Severe Cyclic or API 1104 standards: no tungsten inclusions, no porosity clusters, and zero linear indications.
  • Bend Tests: In mechanical testing, 4 side-bend (or root/face bend) specimens are bent around a plunger through 180 degrees. No tear exceeding 3mm is permitted.

Market Salary Expectations for Certified 6G Welders

Because certified 6G pipe welders represent the pinnacle of industrial metal joining, GCC contractors offer premium compensation:

  • Saudi Arabia (Aramco Approved): SAR 2,500 to SAR 4,200 monthly basic pay, plus food, accommodation, and high overtime hours.
  • Qatar (LNG / Energy Expansion): QAR 2,400 to QAR 3,800 monthly basic.
  • UAE (Offshore Fabrication Yards): AED 2,200 to AED 3,500 monthly.
