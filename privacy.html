Operational note

The public marketing website remains primarily static. Selected operational services, including Wafid/GCC medical booking assistance, securely send required form data to AVC's private ERP/API; payment credentials are handled by Razorpay Checkout.

01 / INFORMATION

Information we may receive

When you contact Assignment Venue Center, apply through an official linked route or use an AVC service, information may include your name, contact details, professional profile, work experience, job preferences and information or documents you choose to provide for the stated service.

02 / MEDICAL SERVICE

Wafid/GCC medical booking assistance

For medical booking assistance, AVC may receive the minimum identity and processing details needed for the case, such as name, email, phone, date of birth, gender, marital status, passport number and passport validity/place details, GCC destination country, preferred medical city, visa type and profession/trade. These details are transmitted to AVC's private operational system and are not intentionally placed in page URLs, analytics events or browser localStorage.

After an AVC operator completes the official Wafid step, the private case may also contain Wafid processing metadata such as Razorpay order/payment references, Wafid slip/reference number, appointed medical center, appointment date, separately incurred Wafid cost, slip file and medical-result status. Candidate identity is not duplicated into the Wafid operational metadata table.

03 / PAYMENT DATA

Razorpay payment data

AVC's medical page asks the private backend to create a fixed ₹1,770 Razorpay Order. Razorpay handles payment authorization. AVC receives payment/order identifiers and verifies the Razorpay signature, captured amount and currency before marking the case paid. AVC does not put passport number in the Razorpay order receipt or notes.

Card number entry, card PIN, CVV, UPI PIN, internet-banking password and payment OTP must only be entered inside Razorpay's secure payment interface. AVC staff and AVC public forms do not ask for those payment credentials.

04 / USE

How information is used

Information may be used for recruitment support, application coordination, interview communication, document-readiness guidance, Wafid/GCC medical booking assistance, payment reconciliation, official slip/status follow-up, employer or recruitment-partner coordination, support, fraud prevention and legally required or legitimate operational record-keeping.

05 / THIRD PARTIES

Third-party services

This website links to or uses services including Razorpay, the official Wafid/Gulf Health Council website, Google Forms, email, WhatsApp, YouTube, Facebook and LinkedIn. Their own privacy terms apply when you use those services. AVC sends candidate data to a third party only where needed for the requested process and subject to applicable verification and law.

06 / MINIMISATION

Data minimisation and failed payments

AVC's current medical flow does not create a medical case merely because a Razorpay payment attempt failed. The failed-payment endpoint receives only limited gateway failure metadata; the candidate/passport form remains in the browser for retry and is not intentionally persisted by that failure event.

Do not send passwords, OTPs, login codes, banking credentials or unnecessary sensitive documents through public contact channels.

07 / ACCESS

Case access and slip retrieval

Public medical status and slip retrieval require an AVC medical reference and matching passport number. These values are submitted in an HTTPS POST request body rather than exposed in the URL. Keep the AVC reference private and report suspected misuse promptly.

08 / SHARING

Sharing

Relevant candidate information may be shared with the official Wafid process, concerned accredited medical center, employer, registered Recruiting Agent or other verified service provider where required for the specific service and subject to applicable law.

09 / SECURITY

Security and retention

AVC uses reasonable technical and organisational controls, including private operational storage, payment verification and access restrictions. No internet or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure. Some records may need to be retained for accounting, tax, compliance, service delivery, fraud prevention or dispute handling; otherwise AVC aims to avoid retaining unnecessary sensitive data.

10 / CHOICES

Your choices

You may contact AVC to request correction, clarification or deletion where legally and operationally applicable. Some records may need to be retained where required for compliance, accounting, dispute handling or legitimate business purposes.

11 / CONTACT

Privacy enquiries

Email privacy enquiries to info@assignmentvenuecentre.me or use the official contact page.
