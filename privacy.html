Operational note

The public marketing and editorial website remains primarily static. Selected operational services may securely send required form data to AVC's private ERP/API; payment credentials are handled by Razorpay Checkout. Google advertising and analytics technologies may also use cookies or similar storage as described below.

01 / INFORMATION

Information we may receive

When you contact Assignment Venue Center, apply through an official linked route or use an AVC service, information may include your name, contact details, professional profile, work experience, job preferences and information or documents you choose to provide for the stated service.

02 / MEDICAL SERVICE

Wafid/GCC medical booking assistance

For medical booking assistance, AVC may receive the minimum identity and processing details needed for the case, such as name, email, phone, date of birth, gender, marital status, passport number and passport validity/place details, GCC destination country, preferred medical city, visa type and profession/trade. These details are transmitted to AVC's private operational system and are not intentionally placed in page URLs, analytics events or browser localStorage.

After an AVC operator completes the official Wafid step, the private case may also contain Wafid processing metadata such as Razorpay order/payment references, Wafid slip/reference number, appointed medical center, appointment date, separately incurred Wafid cost, slip file and medical-result status. Candidate identity is not duplicated into public website content.

03 / PAYMENT DATA

Razorpay payment data

Where the AVC medical service is securely enabled, the private backend creates the published fixed AVC service payment through Razorpay. Razorpay handles payment authorization. AVC receives payment/order identifiers and verifies the Razorpay signature, captured amount and currency before marking a case paid.

Card number entry, card PIN, CVV, UPI PIN, internet-banking password and payment OTP must only be entered inside Razorpay's secure payment interface. AVC staff and AVC public forms do not ask for those payment credentials.

04 / USE

How information is used

Information may be used for recruitment support, application coordination, interview communication, document-readiness guidance, Wafid/GCC medical booking assistance, payment reconciliation, official slip/status follow-up, employer or recruitment-partner coordination, support, fraud prevention and legally required or legitimate operational record-keeping.

05 / THIRD PARTIES

Third-party services

This website links to or uses services including Razorpay, the official Wafid/Gulf Health Council website, Google Forms, Google Analytics or related measurement services, Google AdSense, email, WhatsApp, YouTube, Facebook and LinkedIn. Their own privacy terms apply when you use those services. AVC sends candidate data to a third party only where needed for the requested process and subject to applicable verification and law.

06 / ADVERTISING & COOKIES

Google advertising, cookies and personalization

AVC may use Google AdSense to display advertising on content pages. Third-party vendors, including Google, may use cookies or similar technologies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to this website or other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies may enable Google and its partners to serve personalized ads based on visits to this site and/or other sites on the Internet.

Users can manage or opt out of personalized advertising through Google Ads Settings. Information about how Google uses data when users interact with partner sites is available through Google's published privacy and advertising documentation.

Where legally required, including for relevant users in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, AVC intends to use a Google-certified consent management platform or Google Privacy & messaging solution before serving personalized advertising that requires consent. The consent interface may provide choices regarding ad personalization, cookies/local storage and related data processing.

Advertising is not intended to appear on pages whose primary purpose is payment authorization, private application submission, error/confirmation states or other non-content interactions. AVC may also exclude additional utility pages from monetization.

07 / ANALYTICS

Analytics and measurement

AVC may use privacy-conscious website measurement to understand page performance, traffic sources and navigation. Analytics events are not intended to contain passport numbers, payment credentials, medical results or other sensitive candidate information. Where consent is legally required for analytics storage, the applicable consent mechanism should control that processing.

08 / MINIMISATION

Data minimisation and failed payments

AVC's medical flow is designed not to create a medical case merely because a Razorpay payment attempt failed. Any failed-payment event should contain only limited gateway failure metadata; candidate/passport form data should not be intentionally persisted by that failure event.

Do not send passwords, OTPs, login codes, banking credentials or unnecessary sensitive documents through public contact channels.

09 / ACCESS

Case access and slip retrieval

Where the medical case system is available, public status and slip retrieval require an AVC medical reference and matching passport number. These values should be submitted in an HTTPS POST request body rather than exposed in the URL. Keep the AVC reference private and report suspected misuse promptly.

10 / SHARING

Sharing

Relevant candidate information may be shared with the official Wafid process, concerned accredited medical center, employer, registered Recruiting Agent or other verified service provider where required for the specific service and subject to applicable law.

11 / SECURITY

Security and retention

AVC uses reasonable technical and organisational controls, including private operational storage, payment verification and access restrictions. No internet or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure. Some records may need to be retained for accounting, tax, compliance, service delivery, fraud prevention or dispute handling; otherwise AVC aims to avoid retaining unnecessary sensitive data.

12 / CHOICES

Your choices

You may contact AVC to request correction, clarification or deletion where legally and operationally applicable. Some records may need to be retained where required for compliance, accounting, dispute handling or legitimate business purposes. Advertising choices can also be managed through the applicable Google or consent-message controls described above.

13 / CONTACT

Privacy enquiries

Email privacy enquiries to info@assignmentvenuecentre.me or use the official contact page.
