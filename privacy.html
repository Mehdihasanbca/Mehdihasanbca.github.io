Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 August 2026

This public website does not host a recruitment database or account system. Candidate applications are submitted through the official external application form linked from the website.

Information we may receive

When you contact Assignment Venue Center or submit an application through an official linked form, information may include your name, contact details, professional profile, work experience, job preferences and documents you choose to provide for recruitment support.

How information is used

Information may be used for candidate sourcing, application coordination, interview communication, document-readiness guidance, employer or recruitment-partner coordination, support, fraud prevention and legal or operational record-keeping.

Third-party services

This website links to services such as Google Forms, email, WhatsApp, YouTube, Facebook and LinkedIn. Their own privacy terms apply when you use those services.

Data minimisation

Do not send passwords, OTPs, login codes, banking credentials or unnecessary sensitive documents through public contact channels. AVC limits public website collection to information needed for the stated purpose.

Sharing

Relevant candidate or business information may be shared with concerned employers, registered Recruiting Agents or service providers where required for a specific recruitment-support process and subject to verification and applicable rules.

Security

Reasonable organisational and technical safeguards are used, but no internet transmission can be guaranteed completely secure. Report suspected misuse promptly.

Your choices

You may contact AVC to request correction, clarification or deletion where legally and operationally applicable. Some records may need to be retained for compliance, dispute handling or legitimate business purposes.

Contact

Email privacy enquiries to info@assignmentvenuecentre.me.
