Operational note

The public marketing and editorial website is primarily static. Recruitment enquiries and application routes may send required information to AVC or to an approved external service used for the stated recruitment-support purpose. Google advertising and analytics technologies may use cookies or similar storage as described below.

01 / INFORMATION

Information we may receive

When you contact Assignment Venue Center, apply through an official linked route or use an AVC recruitment-support service, information may include your name, contact details, professional profile, work experience, job preferences and information or documents you choose to provide for the stated purpose.

02 / USE

How information is used

Information may be used for candidate sourcing, application coordination, interview communication, document-readiness guidance, employer or recruitment-partner coordination, support, fraud prevention and legally required or legitimate operational record-keeping.

03 / THIRD PARTIES

Third-party services

This website may link to or use services such as Google Forms, Google Analytics or related measurement services, Google AdSense, email, WhatsApp, YouTube, Facebook and LinkedIn. Their own privacy terms apply when you use those services. AVC shares candidate information with an employer, registered Recruiting Agent or other verified recruitment stakeholder only where needed for the requested process and subject to applicable verification and law.

04 / ADVERTISING & COOKIES

Google advertising, cookies and personalization

AVC may use Google AdSense to display advertising on content pages. Third-party vendors, including Google, may use cookies or similar technologies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to this website or other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies may enable Google and its partners to serve personalized ads based on visits to this site and/or other sites on the Internet.

Users can manage or opt out of personalized advertising through Google Ads Settings. Information about how Google uses data when users interact with partner sites is available through Google's published privacy and advertising documentation.

Where legally required, AVC intends to use a Google-certified consent management platform or Google Privacy & messaging solution before serving personalized advertising that requires consent.

Advertising is not intended to appear on pages whose primary purpose is private application submission, error/confirmation states or other non-content interactions. AVC may also exclude additional utility pages from monetization.

05 / ANALYTICS

Analytics and measurement

AVC may use privacy-conscious website measurement to understand page performance, traffic sources and navigation. Analytics events are not intended to contain passport numbers or other sensitive candidate information. Where consent is legally required for analytics storage, the applicable consent mechanism should control that processing.

06 / MINIMISATION

Data minimisation

AVC aims to collect only information reasonably needed for the stated recruitment-support purpose. Do not send passwords, OTPs, login codes, banking credentials or unnecessary sensitive documents through public contact channels.

07 / SHARING

Sharing

Relevant candidate information may be shared with the concerned employer, registered Recruiting Agent or other verified recruitment stakeholder where required for the specific recruitment process and subject to applicable law.

08 / SECURITY

Security and retention

AVC uses reasonable technical and organisational controls appropriate to its operations. No internet or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure. Some records may need to be retained for compliance, accounting, fraud prevention, service delivery or dispute handling; otherwise AVC aims to avoid retaining unnecessary sensitive data. Historical records from discontinued services may be retained only where a legitimate or legal retention need continues.

09 / CHOICES

Your choices

You may contact AVC to request correction, clarification or deletion where legally and operationally applicable. Some records may need to be retained where required for compliance, accounting, dispute handling or legitimate business purposes. Advertising choices can also be managed through the applicable Google or consent-message controls described above.

10 / CONTACT

Privacy enquiries

Email privacy enquiries to info@assignmentvenuecentre.me or use the official contact page.
