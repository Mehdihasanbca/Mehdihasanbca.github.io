Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 August 2026

This public website does not host a recruitment database or account system. Candidate applications use the official external application route. Selected paid services may use a Razorpay-hosted Payment Page.

Information we may receive

When you contact Assignment Venue Center or submit an application through an official linked form, information may include your name, contact details, professional profile, work experience, job preferences and documents you choose to provide for recruitment support.

Medical booking and Razorpay

For AVC Wafid/GAMCA medical booking assistance, a Razorpay-hosted Payment Page may collect payment contact details such as email and phone together with limited booking-identification information such as full name as per passport, passport number, nationality, GCC destination country, current/medical city, profession or trade and an AVC case reference where available. These fields are used to identify and reconcile the paid booking-assistance case.

Payment-card details, card PIN, CVV, UPI PIN, internet-banking passwords and payment OTPs must only be entered inside Razorpay's secure payment interface. AVC's public website does not ask you to submit those credentials.

How information is used

Information may be used for candidate sourcing, application coordination, interview communication, document-readiness guidance, medical booking assistance, payment reconciliation, employer or recruitment-partner coordination, support, fraud prevention and legal or operational record-keeping.

Third-party services

This website links to or uses services such as Razorpay, Google Forms, email, WhatsApp, YouTube, Facebook and LinkedIn. Their own privacy terms apply when you use those services. Information entered on a Razorpay Payment Page may appear in Razorpay transaction records/reports and receipts available to AVC for payment and service reconciliation.

Data minimisation

Do not send passwords, OTPs, login codes, banking credentials or unnecessary sensitive documents through public contact channels. AVC limits public website and linked-service collection to information needed for the stated purpose.

Sharing

Relevant candidate or business information may be shared with concerned employers, registered Recruiting Agents or service providers where required for a specific recruitment-support or medical-booking process and subject to verification and applicable rules.

Security

Reasonable organisational and technical safeguards are used, but no internet transmission can be guaranteed completely secure. Report suspected misuse promptly.

Your choices

You may contact AVC to request correction, clarification or deletion where legally and operationally applicable. Some records may need to be retained for compliance, accounting, payment reconciliation, dispute handling or legitimate business purposes.

Contact

Email privacy enquiries to info@assignmentvenuecentre.me.
