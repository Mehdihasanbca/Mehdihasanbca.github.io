01 / PARTNER RECORD

A partnership review starts with identity and authorization—not candidate volume.

Provide facts that let AVC understand who is requesting candidates and how the requirement is authorized.

Legal identity

Agency / company legal name, country and authorized contact person.

Use business contact details that can be tied to the organization.

Licence / authority

Recruitment licence, registration or authorization details where legally applicable.

A sourcing partner should not be presented as the regulated Recruiting Agent when the law requires a licensed role.

Demand source

Employer / project information and written requirement context appropriate for review.

Clarify whether the partner is acting directly, under authorization, or through another recruitment stakeholder.

Positions

Job titles, quantity, experience/skill criteria and target sourcing geography.

Separate trades and selection criteria so profiles can be matched correctly.

Employment terms

Salary, duty hours, contract period, accommodation, transport, food/allowance and other benefits.

Candidate briefing must be based on the confirmed terms, not assumptions or unofficial variations.

Selection route

CV screening, interview/trade test method, decision-maker and expected schedule.

Define how confirmed results will be communicated before mobilization begins.

Processing responsibility

Visa, emigration, travel, deployment and applicable Recruiting Agent responsibilities.

These responsibilities must remain with the legally responsible employer / recruitment stakeholders.

Commercial scope

Proposed sourcing/coordination model, payment trigger and operational expectations.

Commercial discussions should follow verified requirement facts and clearly assigned responsibilities.

02 / CONTROL POINTS

AVC should know what is verified before candidate sourcing starts.

Partner should provide

  • Business identity and authorized contact
  • Licence / authorization details where applicable
  • Employer/project and demand information appropriate for review
  • Confirmed job terms and selection procedure
  • Responsible visa, emigration, travel and deployment route
  • Commercial proposal and payment responsibility

AVC can coordinate after review

  • Candidate sourcing against confirmed criteria
  • Profile and document readiness coordination
  • Work/introduction video collection when genuinely required
  • Interview scheduling and candidate communication
  • Candidate-to-requirement organization and handoff
  • Operational follow-up with the concerned stakeholders
