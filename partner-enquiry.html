Share the demand and authorization route before candidate mobilization.

AVC uses a structured email route instead of a public partner-registration backend. This keeps the first enquiry transparent and lets AVC review agency identity, requirement facts and responsibility boundaries before sourcing work begins.

Partner enquiry checklist

  • Agency / legal business identity.
  • Country and licence / authorization details where applicable.
  • Written demand or requirement summary.
  • Employer / project information appropriate for review.
  • Positions, quantity, salary and benefits.
  • Interview / selection procedure.
  • Visa, emigration, travel and deployment responsibility.
  • Commercial and coordination proposal.
