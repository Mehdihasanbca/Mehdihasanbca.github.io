⚖️ The AVC Sathi Ethical Charter (Mandatory Compliance)

Every partner must agree to the statutory requirements of the Indian Emigration Act 1983:

  • Strict ₹0 Candidate Fee: No partner shall charge, demand, or accept any money, advance, or commission from candidates for registration, trade testing, or interviews.
  • Authentic Trade Representation: Only candidates with genuine practical skills in their stated trade will be presented for testing.
  • Document Integrity: Zero tolerance for forged certificates, fake experience letters, or altered passports.
  • Direct Operational Coordination: Institutional incentives and coordination logistics are handled directly between AVC and the partner organization, completely independent of the candidate.
