01 / REQUIREMENT MATRIX

A sourcing brief should answer the operational questions before candidates are mobilized.

Use the right-hand column as a quality check when preparing the requirement.

Company identity

Legal company / organization name and authorized contact person.

Use an email domain, company record or other business identity suitable for verification.

Project / location

Country, city/project, worksite context and sector where relevant.

Location affects sourcing, travel, interview and candidate briefing.

Roles / quantity

Exact job titles and number of workers required for each role.

Separate different trades instead of combining them into one vague manpower count.

Criteria

Experience, trade skills, certifications, language or other genuine selection criteria.

Criteria should be job-related and clear enough for candidate screening.

Employment terms

Salary, duty hours, overtime basis, contract period and benefits.

State accommodation, transport, food/allowance and other material terms separately.

Selection

Interview method, trade test, document review and target schedule.

AVC can coordinate logistics; final selection remains with the concerned decision-maker.

Recruitment route

Responsible employer / registered Recruiting Agent and authorization route where legally applicable.

Clarify visa, emigration, travel and deployment responsibility before mobilization.

02 / RESPONSIBILITY MAP

Separate employer decisions from AVC coordination work.

Employer / authorized stakeholder provides

  • Genuine manpower requirement and employment terms
  • Selection criteria and final hiring decision
  • Project, worksite and joining requirements
  • Applicable authorization / Recruiting Agent route
  • Formal visa, emigration, travel and deployment responsibilities as applicable

AVC can coordinate

  • Requirement structuring and sourcing brief clarification
  • Candidate sourcing and initial profile organization
  • CV, document and work-video readiness where requested
  • Interview scheduling and candidate communication
  • Recruitment-support handoffs with the concerned stakeholders
03 / OPERATING FLOW

A requirement should move through four controlled stages.

01 / RECEIVE

Structured brief

AVC receives the company, role, quantity, terms and recruitment-route information.

02 / REVIEW

Clarify gaps

Missing role, commercial, interview or responsibility details are clarified before broad sourcing.

03 / SOURCE

Candidate coordination

Relevant candidate profiles can be sourced and organized against the confirmed requirement.

04 / HANDOFF

Selection process

Shortlisted candidates proceed into the confirmed interview and authorized recruitment route.
