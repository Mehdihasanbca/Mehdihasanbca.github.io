Visit policy

Confirm your appointment before travelling.

The office address above is AVC's verified principal place of business. Interview venues and timings can vary by specific recruitment requirement.

Before travel: confirm the vacancy/interview reference, date, time and exact venue through official AVC/recruitment communication. Do not travel only on the basis of a forwarded social-media message.
Interview-day guidance

Before attending an AVC-coordinated interview.

Bring / prepare

  • The vacancy or interview reference
  • Current CV and role information
  • Requested identity / experience documents only
  • Work or introduction video when specifically required
  • Official appointment or interview communication

Verify first

  • The job and interview details
  • The concerned employer or recruitment stakeholder
  • Any document request
  • Any payment request and written terms
  • The exact venue and schedule
