Office & interview support

A professional space for candidate assistance and recruitment coordination.

AVC's office activity is focused on candidate reception, interview coordination, document-readiness guidance and operational meetings. Public information is limited to verified details.

Business base: Darbhanga, Bihar, India
Office role: recruitment support and interview coordination

Office functions

What the AVC office can support.

Candidate reception

Guidance for registration, scheduled interviews, profile checks and recruitment-process communication.

Physical interviews

Coordination support when an employer or recruitment stakeholder schedules a physical interview session.

Video interviews

Candidate communication and basic setup coordination for online employer or recruitment-partner interviews.

Document guidance

Checklist-based readiness support for documents requested in a verified recruitment process.

Employer / partner meetings

Operational discussion of manpower requirements, candidate sourcing and recruitment-support coordination.

Internal operations

Candidate, employer and partner follow-up related to active recruitment-support work.

Visit policy

Use the exact venue shared for your specific appointment or interview.

AVC is based in Darbhanga, Bihar. A specific street address or interview venue should be taken from official communication for the scheduled event.

Why no invented address: the public website will not publish an unverified street address, map pin, phone number or office photograph. Exact venue information will be added only from AVC-approved source details.
Interview-day guidance

Before attending an AVC-coordinated interview.

Bring / prepare

  • The vacancy or interview reference
  • Current CV and role information
  • Requested identity / experience documents only
  • Work or introduction video when specifically required
  • Official appointment or interview communication

Verify first

  • The job and interview details
  • The concerned employer or recruitment stakeholder
  • Any document request
  • Any payment request and written terms
  • The exact venue and schedule
Important boundary

Office support does not change AVC's recruitment role.

Public position: AVC provides recruitment support and interview coordination. Final selection, recruitment approval, visa processing, emigration and overseas deployment are handled by the concerned employer and/or registered Recruiting Agent, subject to applicable law and verification.
