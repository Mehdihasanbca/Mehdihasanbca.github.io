Compliance & Candidate Safety

Clear Operating Boundaries. Zero Misleading Claims.

To ensure 100% legal compliance with the Indian Emigration Act, AVC clearly states what we coordinate and what remains with authorized employers and licensed recruiting agents.

✓ What AVC Coordinates

  • ✓ Candidate Sourcing: Reaching out to skilled tradespeople against verified manpower demands.
  • ✓ Application Coordination: Collecting candidate profiles through official free application routes.
  • ✓ CV & Profile Support: Helping candidates format their genuine skills, work experience, and trade backgrounds.
  • ✓ Document Readiness: Providing clear checklists for genuine documents required by specific employers.
  • ✓ Interview Coordination: Providing scheduling, physical venue arrangements, and interview readiness support.
  • ✓ Partner Follow-up: Communicating demand requirements with corporate employers and licensed agents.

❌ What AVC Does NOT Do

  • ❌ No Visa Issuance: We do not issue visas, permits, or government emigration clearances directly.
  • ❌ No Final Hiring Authority: Selection decisions are made exclusively by the responsible employer.
  • ❌ No Guaranteed Employment: Registering with AVC does not guarantee selection or overseas placement.
  • ❌ No Unofficial Fees: We never charge candidate fees for job interviews or hall tickets.
  • ❌ No Document Withholding: We never withhold original passports or educational certificates.
  • ❌ No Personal Payments: We never accept payments into personal UPI or individual bank accounts.
