What AVC does

Recruitment support built around real operational needs.

AVC focuses on coordination, preparation and communication rather than unsupported promises.

01

Candidate sourcing

Profile collection, requirement-based sourcing and coordination with candidates interested in available opportunities.

02

Application coordination

Structured application support, vacancy communication and follow-up with candidates and recruitment stakeholders.

03

CV and profile support

Guidance for clearer CVs, role-relevant information and interview-ready candidate presentation.

04

Document readiness

Checklist-based guidance for documents requested during recruitment, subject to employer and agency requirements.

05

Interview coordination

Physical or online interview scheduling support, candidate communication and interview-day coordination.

06

Employer and agency support

Manpower requirement coordination, candidate sharing, communication support and process follow-up.
