About AVC

A working recruitment-support business, not a promise page.

Assignment Venue Center works on the coordination side of recruitment: collecting suitable profiles, organizing applications, preparing candidates for interviews, guiding document readiness and supporting communication between recruitment stakeholders.

AVC is based in Darbhanga, Bihar and supports Indian candidates along with employers and recruitment partners handling verified manpower requirements.

Candidate process

From application to authorized processing.

  1. 01

    Register

    Submit accurate profile and contact details through the official AVC application route.

  2. 02

    Requirement review

    Your profile is considered against the available job criteria and recruitment requirement.

  3. 03

    Interview coordination

    Eligible candidates receive the available interview instructions, timing and preparation information.

  4. 04

    Document readiness

    Selected candidates follow the document checklist required for that employer or Recruiting Agent process.

  5. 05

    Authorized processing

    Visa, emigration, travel and deployment continue through the concerned authorized stakeholders.
