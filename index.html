Workflow / Candidate

Five handoffs from profile to authorized processing.

  1. 01

    Register

    Submit accurate profile and contact details through the official AVC route.

  2. 02

    Requirement match

    Profile is considered against available job criteria.

  3. 03

    Interview

    Eligible candidates receive interview timing, method and preparation information.

  4. 04

    Document readiness

    Selected candidates follow the checklist required for that recruitment process.

  5. 05

    Authorized processing

    Employer / Recruiting Agent continues formal processing, visa, travel and deployment.
