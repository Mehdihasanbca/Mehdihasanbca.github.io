Trade Test

A trade test is a practical skill demonstration in which a candidate performs their craft under assessment conditions. It is evaluated by a qualified examiner, often with the employer's technical delegation present. The test simulates real job-site conditions and determines whether a candidate meets the employer's minimum competency standard.

AVC hosts trade tests at its Darbhanga facility with proper equipment bays for electrical wiring, welding, HVAC, and driving assessments.

Documents

For initial registration, you need:

  • Valid passport (ECNR status preferred — see below)
  • Recent passport-size photographs (4–6 copies)
  • Trade certificates or experience letters
  • CV or bio-data with trade and experience details

After selection, additional documents required include: GAMCA medical clearance, police clearance certificate, eMigrate registration, and signed employment contract.

Indian passports carry either an ECR (Emigration Check Required) or ECNR (Emigration Check Not Required) stamp.

  • ECR passports require eMigrate clearance before travel to Gulf countries. The process adds steps but provides government oversight and consular protection.
  • ECNR passports do not require eMigrate clearance, though voluntary registration is recommended for safety.

Most candidates with 10th-pass qualifications or above, or with professional certificates, qualify for ECNR. Read our complete ECNR vs ECR guide for how to check and upgrade your status.
