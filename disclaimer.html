Important notice

Recruitment Disclaimer

Understand AVC's role before proceeding.

Assignment Venue Center is presented on this website as a recruitment-support and coordination service. This website does not represent AVC as an embassy, visa authority, government department or guaranteed job provider.

Final decisions

Employers and authorized recruitment stakeholders make final shortlisting and selection decisions. Government authorities decide visa, emigration and immigration matters.

Vacancy information

Job details may change, close, be placed on hold or be withdrawn by the concerned employer or agency. Candidates should confirm the latest approved information before taking action.

Timelines

Recruitment, medical, visa, emigration, travel and deployment timelines vary. No fixed completion date is guaranteed unless confirmed in a binding document by the authorized responsible party.

Payments

Do not make a payment based only on an unofficial message or personal request. Verify the purpose, recipient, receipt terms, refund conditions and responsible recruitment entity before payment.

Documents

Candidates remain responsible for the accuracy and authenticity of documents they submit. False or altered documents may result in rejection and legal consequences.

Report concerns

Report suspicious communication to info@assignmentvenuecentre.me and review the Fraud Safety guidance.
