People-first recruitment information

Practical guides for decisions that matter before an overseas job begins.

These guides turn AVC's day-to-day recruitment-support work into clear, reusable information for candidates, employers and recruitment partners. We distinguish verified facts from vacancy-specific terms, link to official sources where rules are involved, and update pages when a process changes.

How this section is different

It is not a vacancy feed and it is not written to chase search trends. Each guide answers a recurring real-world recruitment question and shows the checks, documents or decisions a reader can use immediately.
