Operating model

Clear responsibility at every stage.

AVC operates as a recruitment-support and sourcing partner. Overseas recruitment authorization, visa/emigration processing and final deployment remain with the concerned authorized stakeholders.

AVC supports

  • Candidate sourcing and application intake
  • Requirement-based screening coordination
  • CV/profile preparation guidance
  • Online and physical interview coordination
  • Document checklist and process guidance
  • Communication between candidates and recruitment stakeholders
  • Employer and recruitment-partner manpower coordination

AVC does not represent

  • A guaranteed job, selection, visa or overseas deployment
  • Embassy authorization unless specifically evidenced for a particular process
  • Government recruitment status unless a specific verified authorization exists
  • An independent overseas Recruiting Agent role where such licence/registration is legally required
  • A trade-test or skill-certification centre unless separately authorized for that activity

Recruitment boundary: Final selection, recruitment approval, visa processing, emigration and overseas deployment are handled by the concerned employer and/or registered Recruiting Agent, subject to applicable law, documentation and verification.

How AVC works

A structured recruitment-support workflow.

Requirement

A manpower requirement is received through an employer, registered RA or recruitment partner.

Sourcing

AVC communicates the requirement and collects suitable candidate applications.

Screening

Candidate profiles are organized against available job criteria.

Interview

Interview communication and coordination is arranged for eligible candidates.

Authorized processing

Selected candidates continue through employer/RA documentation, visa, emigration and deployment procedures.
