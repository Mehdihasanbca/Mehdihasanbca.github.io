Working model

Each party keeps a clear responsibility.

AVC focuses on sourcing and recruitment support. Regulated overseas recruitment steps remain with the employer and/or licensed Recruiting Agent responsible for that process.

AVC coordinates

  • Candidate sourcing and profile collection
  • Application and screening coordination
  • CV/profile readiness
  • Interview communication
  • Document checklist guidance
  • Employer and partner follow-up

Authorized recruitment process handles

  • Final selection and recruitment approval
  • Visa and emigration processing where applicable
  • Formal employment documentation
  • Travel and overseas deployment
  • Government or embassy procedures requiring specific authorization

Important: Applying through AVC does not guarantee selection, visa approval or deployment. Final decisions depend on the specific employer, Recruiting Agent and applicable processing requirements.
