Payment & refund policy

Clear rules before money changes hands.

This policy explains how AVC handles service payments, failed or duplicate transactions, cancellations and refunds. Third-party charges already paid for a candidate's verified process may have different refund rules from AVC's own service charge.

Keep your records.

Retain the AVC case/reference number, fee summary, Razorpay order/payment reference, receipt and any booking/slip reference. Refund review is based on the verified transaction and the work/cost already incurred.

Before payment

What AVC should disclose.

Service information

  • What AVC is being paid to assist with.
  • The AVC case/reference number where applicable.
  • The amount payable and the current fee breakup.
  • Any known third-party charge included in or separate from AVC's fee.
  • The expected next step after payment.

Customer responsibility

  • Review candidate/payer details and service description before paying.
  • Use only official AVC payment routes.
  • Do not share PIN, CVV, UPI PIN, banking password or payment OTP with AVC staff.
  • Report an incorrect or duplicate payment promptly through official support.
Cancellation & refund treatment

Refund depends on the stage of the service.

Before work/third-party cost starts

If AVC has not started the requested service and no third-party charge has been incurred, a cancellation request can be reviewed for refund of the unused amount.

After AVC work has started

If AVC has already performed material booking/coordination work, the refundable amount may exclude the reasonable service portion already performed, subject to the facts of the case and applicable law.

After third-party fee is paid

If AVC has already paid Wafid or another third party for the candidate's approved process, that third-party amount is subject to the third party's own cancellation/refund rules and is not automatically refundable by AVC.

Payment exceptions

Failed, duplicate and incorrect payments.

Failed / pending transaction

A browser success/failure message alone is not final. AVC should rely on server-side Razorpay verification and settlement/order status before treating a case as paid or unpaid.

Duplicate payment

If the same approved AVC case is genuinely paid more than once, AVC will reconcile the Razorpay references and review the duplicate amount for refund, after confirming that it was not used for a separate service or third-party cost.

Wrong amount or wrong case

Contact AVC immediately with the payment reference. Do not make another payment until the first transaction is checked.

Refund credit timing

Once AVC approves and initiates a refund through the applicable payment route, the final credit timing depends on the payment provider, bank/card network and payment method. AVC will not promise a shorter bank-credit time than the provider can support.

Wafid medical assistance

Special third-party boundary.

Wafid/GAMCA: AVC's role is booking assistance and coordination. Medical fitness is decided by the authorized medical process, not AVC. A completed Wafid booking, medical examination, visa decision or deployment outcome is not guaranteed by paying AVC.

Buyer protection claims

No self-created “money back” badge.

AVC will not state that a transaction is protected by Razorpay Buyer Protection or Money Back Promise unless Razorpay actually marks that merchant/transaction as eligible under its own program. Razorpay independently determines claim eligibility.

Request a payment review

Use the official company channel.

Email info@assignmentvenuecentre.me with the AVC case reference, Razorpay payment/order reference, amount, date and a short description of the issue. Do not email card numbers, CVV, PIN, UPI PIN, banking passwords or OTPs.
