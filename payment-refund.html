Payment & refund policy

AVC service fee and third-party cost are kept separate.

For Wafid/GCC medical booking assistance, AVC's service fee is ₹1,500 plus 18% GST. The official Wafid/third-party booking amount is separate and handled at actual cost when applicable.

Keep your payment records.

Pure-agent treatment

Exact Wafid reimbursement is not automatically GST-free.

AVC will use pure-agent treatment only when the actual transaction satisfies the applicable Rule 33 conditions, including candidate authorization, separate invoice disclosure and recovery of only the exact third-party amount without markup. If those conditions are not satisfied, tax treatment can differ.

Cancellation & refund

Refund depends on what has already happened.

Before service starts

If AVC has not started material work and no third-party amount has been incurred, a cancellation request can be reviewed for the unused service amount, subject to applicable law.

After AVC work starts

If material booking/coordination work has already been performed, the refundable service portion depends on the actual work completed and applicable law.

After Wafid cost is incurred

An actual Wafid/third-party amount already paid for the candidate's approved case is subject to that third party's own cancellation/refund rules. AVC will not describe that amount as its own service revenue.

Payment exceptions

Failed, duplicate and reconciliation cases.

Failed / pending

A failed Razorpay attempt does not create a paid AVC medical case. The current medical flow does not persist candidate/passport form data merely because the payment failed.

Duplicate payment

The private medical API prevents duplicate case creation for the same verified Razorpay payment ID. A true duplicate charge can be reconciled from Razorpay payment references and reviewed for refund.

Payment succeeded, case missing

Do not pay again. Contact AVC with the Razorpay Payment ID so the transaction can be reconciled against the server-side order and candidate details.

Refund credit timing

After an approved refund is initiated, final bank/card credit timing depends on Razorpay, the issuing bank and payment method.

No self-created protection claim

Razorpay Buyer Protection / Money Back Promise is shown only if Razorpay actually enables it.

AVC does not create its own Razorpay trust or money-back badge.
