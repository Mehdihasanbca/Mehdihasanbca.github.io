Requirement brief

Information that helps AVC source accurately.

A clear written requirement reduces candidate confusion and improves screening quality.

Job requirement details

  • Company / project / work location
  • Job title and number of positions
  • Required experience and trade criteria
  • Salary, duty hours and benefits
  • Accommodation, transport and food terms
  • Interview method and target date
  • Required documents, videos or tests

Process / authorization details

  • Recruitment route and responsible registered RA where applicable
  • Demand / authorization documents appropriate to the process
  • Selection and offer terms
  • Visa / processing responsibility
  • Any candidate fees or costs must be lawful, clear and documented
  • Deployment timeline should be presented as an estimate, not a guarantee
