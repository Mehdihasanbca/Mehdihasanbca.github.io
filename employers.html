Requirement brief

The information that helps sourcing start correctly.

A complete job brief reduces candidate confusion and avoids unnecessary screening.

Job details

  • Company / project / work location
  • Job title and number of positions
  • Required experience and trade criteria
  • Salary, duty hours and benefits
  • Accommodation, transport and food terms
  • Interview method and target date
  • Required documents, videos or tests

Processing details

  • Recruitment route and responsible Recruiting Agent where applicable
  • Selection and offer terms
  • Visa / processing responsibility
  • Candidate fees or costs, if any, should be lawful and documented
  • Expected deployment timeline and known dependencies
