Does AVC charge any fee from job candidates for registration, trade testing, or interviews?

No. Assignment Venue Center follows a strict ₹0 candidate fee policy. In full compliance with the Indian Emigration Act 1983, candidate sourcing, application registration, technical trade pre-screening, and interview attendance at our Darbhanga venue are 100% free for jobseekers. AVC never charges candidates any fees, deposits, or visa advances. Anyone asking for money claiming to represent AVC is committing fraud.
