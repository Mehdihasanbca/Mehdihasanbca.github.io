01 / SCOPE

What moves through this service.

AVC receives

  • Job title, quantity and work location
  • Experience, trade and eligibility criteria
  • Salary and benefits approved for candidate communication
  • Interview or screening method
  • Responsible employer / recruitment contact

AVC coordinates

  • Requirement-based outreach
  • Candidate profile collection
  • Experience and availability checks from supplied information
  • Profile organization for review
  • Shortlist and follow-up communication

Responsibility handoff

Support stops where authorized processing begins.

AVC handles candidate sourcing and coordination. Final selection, visa/emigration processing and overseas deployment remain with the concerned employer and/or licensed Recruiting Agent.
