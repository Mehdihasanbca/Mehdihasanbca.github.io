Service 01

Candidate sourcing

AVC supports requirement-based candidate outreach, profile collection and initial matching for verified manpower needs.

Best for: employers and recruitment partners who need organized candidate sourcing support.

What AVC does

  • Receive role, quantity, location and eligibility information
  • Source interested candidate profiles against stated requirements
  • Collect role-relevant experience and availability information
  • Coordinate profile sharing and initial follow-up
  • Help organize candidate lists for screening or interview

What AVC needs

  • Clear job title and manpower quantity
  • Country / project location
  • Experience and eligibility criteria
  • Salary and benefits where approved for communication
  • Interview or screening method
  • Employer / registered RA coordination details as applicable
Workflow

From requirement to candidate shortlist.

1

Requirement intake

AVC receives and reviews the manpower requirement.

2

Sourcing

Relevant candidates are invited or identified.

3

Profile readiness

Available candidate information is organized for review.

4

Share & follow-up

Profiles are coordinated with the concerned stakeholder for next steps.

AVC sourcing support does not itself constitute final overseas recruitment, visa processing or deployment approval. Those responsibilities remain with the concerned employer and/or registered Recruiting Agent.
