Service 03

CV & profile support

AVC helps candidates present work history, trade experience and role-relevant information in a clearer, interview-ready format.

Goal: clearer candidate presentation without inventing experience, qualifications or achievements.

What AVC can improve

  • Work-history structure and chronology
  • Trade / job title clarity
  • Experience responsibilities and role keywords
  • Contact and basic profile presentation
  • Interview-ready summary of relevant experience

What AVC will not do

  • Invent employers or years of experience
  • Create false certificates or qualifications
  • Add skills the candidate does not actually have
  • Misrepresent identity, age, nationality or work history
Profile readiness

Information candidates should provide accurately.

1

Identity

Name and current contact details.

2

Work history

Employer, role and actual dates.

3

Skills

Trade skills the candidate can demonstrate.

4

Supporting proof

Relevant documents when legitimately available.
