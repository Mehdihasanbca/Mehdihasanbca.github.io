01 / PROFILE RECORD

Every useful CV starts with a small set of clear, consistent records.

Identity

Name, current mobile/WhatsApp, email, nationality and current location.

Keep spelling and identity details consistent with genuine documents.

Target role

Use the trade or position the candidate can actually perform.

Avoid combining unrelated titles only to match more vacancies.

Experience

Total experience can be summarized, but each employer entry should still show its actual period.

Do not extend dates to remove gaps or increase experience.

Responsibilities

List the real tasks, tools, equipment, systems or work environment relevant to the role.

Prefer specific duties over generic claims such as “all work knowledge”.

Qualifications

Include education, trade training or certificates that are genuinely held and relevant.

Never add a certificate or licence that cannot be produced when requested.

02 / WORK HISTORY

Employment chronology should be readable without guesswork.

For each genuine employer or project, keep the same five-point pattern.

01Employer / project

Use the genuine company, contractor or project name as applicable.

02Job title

Use the title that best reflects the actual work performed.

03Period

Month/year to month/year, based on the real employment period.

04Location

City and country where the work was performed when known and relevant.

05Role evidence

Describe practical duties, tools, equipment, machines, systems, products or responsibilities the candidate can explain in an interview.

03 / ROLE EVIDENCE

Work and introduction videos should support the actual vacancy—not replace the CV.

INTRODUCTION VIDEO

Keep it short and factual.

  • Name and current location
  • Target trade / position
  • Relevant years and type of experience
  • Current or recent work responsibilities
  • Language requested by the verified vacancy, when applicable
WORK / TRADE VIDEO

Demonstrate only work the candidate can genuinely perform.

  • Use a safe workplace and appropriate protective equipment
  • Show role-relevant tools or tasks when permitted
  • Avoid staged claims or edited proof of skills
  • Do not expose confidential employer information
  • Submit a video only when the verified requirement asks for one
04 / QUALITY GATE

A recruiter should be able to scan the profile and understand the candidate quickly.

Before sharing

  • Contact details are current
  • Dates do not contradict each other
  • Target role matches demonstrated skills
  • Responsibilities use clear, simple language
  • Relevant qualifications can be produced when requested
  • File is readable on a phone as well as desktop

Keep separate until requested

  • Full sensitive identity-document copies
  • Unrelated certificates and personal records
  • Medical or processing documents for a stage not yet applicable
  • Any document requested through an unverified channel
  • Private employer information not needed for recruitment review
05 / COMMON ERRORS

Fix presentation problems without changing the underlying facts.

ERROR / 01

Conflicting dates

Employment periods, total experience and passport-age timeline should not contradict each other.

ERROR / 02

Generic duties

Replace vague descriptions with genuine role-specific responsibilities the candidate can explain.

ERROR / 03

Unsupported title

Do not promote a helper, technician or supervisor title beyond the work actually performed.

ERROR / 04

Too much unrelated data

Keep the profile focused on the vacancy instead of attaching every available record.

ERROR / 05

Fabricated proof

Never create false employers, experience letters, qualifications, licences, certificates or work evidence.

ERROR / 06

Unverified sharing

Do not send sensitive profile documents to unknown numbers, accounts or links without checking the recruitment route.

06 / HANDOFF

A strong profile supports screening; it does not create a selection decision.

AVC can support factual profile presentation, application coordination and interview readiness. Final selection and applicable overseas recruitment, visa/emigration and deployment steps remain with the concerned employer and/or registered Recruiting Agent.
