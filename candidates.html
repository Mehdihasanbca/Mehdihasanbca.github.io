Prepare responsibly

Typical information you may be asked to keep ready.

Exact requirements differ by role and country; submit only what is requested through a verified process.

Profile readiness

  • Valid contact details
  • Current CV / work history
  • Passport details when required
  • Trade / role experience information
  • Work or introduction video when specifically requested
  • Education or skill documents relevant to the job

Important safety rules

  • Do not rely on guaranteed job or visa promises
  • Verify job reference, employer and registered RA details where applicable
  • Keep written terms and receipts for any legitimate payment
  • Do not share sensitive documents with unknown social-media accounts
  • Use AVC's official website, email and listed channels

Important: Registration with AVC does not guarantee selection, visa approval or overseas deployment. Final decisions depend on the concerned employer, registered Recruiting Agent and applicable processing requirements.
