Before you apply

Keep the basic information ready.

Only submit documents that are relevant to the actual vacancy and requested process.

Profile readiness

  • Valid contact details
  • Current CV / work history
  • Passport details when required
  • Trade / role experience information
  • Work or introduction video when requested
  • Relevant education or skill documents

Protect your information

  • Use AVC's official website and listed channels
  • Check role, salary, location and selection method before applying
  • Verify uncertain payment or document requests
  • Keep written terms and receipts for legitimate payments
  • Avoid unknown accounts asking for sensitive documents

Selection: Registration does not guarantee a job, visa approval or deployment. Final decisions depend on the employer, responsible Recruiting Agent and applicable recruitment process.
