02 / PROCESS

The candidate journey is a sequence, not a guarantee.

The exact order can vary by employer, country, vacancy and responsible recruitment stakeholder.

01

Review vacancy

Check the role, location, terms, vacancy reference and validity before acting.

02

Apply accurately

Use factual contact, profile and experience information through the official application route.

03

Screen & prepare

AVC may coordinate matching, communication, document readiness and interview preparation.

04

Interview / assessment

Follow confirmed instructions. Attendance, shortlisting or document collection itself does not mean selection.

05

Formal processing

Selected candidates continue with the concerned employer and/or registered Recruiting Agent for applicable formalities.

03 / READINESS

Keep a factual candidate pack ready.

Requirements vary by vacancy; not every item is required in every case.

Core profile information

  • Current contact details
  • Factual CV and work history
  • Trade, role and experience information
  • Education or skill records when relevant
  • Passport details when the verified process requires them

Vacancy-specific items

  • Work sample or trade video when requested
  • Introduction video when requested
  • Interview timing and joining instructions
  • Country or recruitment-route requirements
  • Written terms and legitimate receipts where applicable
04 / VERIFY

Protect the route before you protect the speed.

Urgency is not a substitute for source, vacancy or document verification.
