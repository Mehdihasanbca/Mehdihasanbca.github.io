01 / WARNING SIGNALS

Five patterns deserve immediate verification.

One warning sign does not automatically prove fraud, but it is enough reason to stop and check before proceeding.

FLAG / 01

Guarantee

Guaranteed job, guaranteed visa or guaranteed deployment without a real selection and authorized process.

FLAG / 02

Pressure

“Pay now,” “send documents immediately” or threats that the opportunity will disappear if you verify first.

FLAG / 03

Hidden payment

Requests to transfer money without a clear purpose, written terms, legitimate recipient details or receipt.

FLAG / 04

Credential request

Requests for OTPs, passwords, candidate login codes, full card details or online-banking access.

FLAG / 05

Copied identity

AVC logo or name used on an unknown domain, account or profile that does not match published AVC routes.

02 / RESPONSE PROTOCOL

Use a fixed response sequence instead of negotiating with the suspicious sender.

01 / STOP

Pause the action

Do not pay, send new sensitive documents or share security credentials while the request is unclear.

02 / PRESERVE

Keep evidence

Save the message, sender details, phone number, payment instruction, link and vacancy reference if available.

03 / CROSS-CHECK

Use official sources

Compare the claim with AVC's website, current vacancy page, company record and published contact routes.

04 / VERIFY

Contact AVC directly

Use the official phone or email independently of the suspicious message to ask whether the request is genuine.

DO NOT SEND

Passwords, OTPs, full banking credentials or unnecessary sensitive documents should not be included in a fraud-verification email.

04 / DOCUMENT & PAYMENT SAFETY

Protect the two areas that create the most irreversible exposure.

Document safety

  • Share passport or identity files only when relevant to a verified recruitment stage
  • Use known AVC, employer or registered Recruiting Agent routes as applicable
  • Do not alter or fabricate records to meet a vacancy requirement
  • Keep your own copy of important documents submitted
  • Avoid sending an entire personal-document folder when only one item is requested

Payment safety

  • Understand exactly what the payment is for
  • Verify recipient name/account and applicable terms
  • Expect a legitimate record or receipt where payment is valid
  • Do not pay only because a message creates urgency or fear
  • Never share OTPs, passwords or full banking access to “complete” a recruitment payment
05 / REPORT PACKAGE

Send enough context for AVC to understand the issue without exposing more private data.

INCLUDE / 01

Sender details

Phone number, email, account/profile name or website/domain used in the suspicious communication.

INCLUDE / 02

Vacancy context

Job title, country and AVC vacancy/reference information if the message claims to relate to a published opportunity.

INCLUDE / 03

Request details

What the sender asked you to pay, share, click or do, including the claimed purpose.

INCLUDE / 04

Relevant evidence

Screenshot or copied message text that shows the suspicious claim without adding unrelated private information.

EXCLUDE / 05

Security secrets

Do not send OTPs, passwords, PINs, full card details or online-banking credentials.
