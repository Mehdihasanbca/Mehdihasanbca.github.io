Candidate protection

Fraud Safety

Verify first. Share carefully. Never rely on a guarantee.

AVC does not guarantee selection, visa approval or deployment. Treat promises of a guaranteed overseas job or visa as a warning sign.

Use official sources

  • Official website: assignmentvenuecentre.me
  • Official email: info@assignmentvenuecentre.me
  • Use only social and application links published on the official website.

Before applying

  • Check the job title, country, salary, benefits, contract period and responsible recruitment entity.
  • Ask for a vacancy or demand reference where available.
  • Confirm whether the employer or registered Recruiting Agent is responsible for final recruitment and deployment.

Before making a payment

  • Verify the recipient name, service purpose, amount, receipt and refund terms.
  • Do not transfer money because of urgency, pressure or a personal message alone.
  • Do not share OTPs, passwords, candidate login codes or banking credentials.

Protect documents

  • Share passport and identity documents only when required for a verified process.
  • Do not send sensitive documents to unknown personal accounts.
  • Keep copies of forms, receipts, agreements and communication.

Common warning signs

  • Guaranteed selection or visa.
  • Requests to hide payments or avoid receipts.
  • Use of copied AVC logos on unofficial domains or social profiles.
  • Pressure to act immediately without verification.
  • Requests for passwords, OTPs or full banking access.

Report suspicious activity

Forward suspicious communication, payment instructions or copied branding to info@assignmentvenuecentre.me. Do not include passwords, OTPs or unnecessary sensitive documents.
