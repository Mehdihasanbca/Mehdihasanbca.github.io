  • Full Name as per Passport

Booking requirement

  • GCC Destination Country

  • AVC Case Reference, if already issued
Payment security: do not enter card PIN, CVV, UPI PIN, internet-banking password or payment OTP in any onboarding field. Payment credentials belong only inside Razorpay's secure checkout.
How it works

Hosted payment, manual Wafid booking.

1

Open AVC Razorpay page

Candidate opens the official Razorpay-hosted AVC Medical Booking Assistance page from this website.

2

Complete onboarding

Candidate enters the required identity and booking fields on Razorpay.

3

Pay ₹1,770

Razorpay processes the AVC service payment and provides the payment/receipt reference.

4

AVC verifies

AVC checks the Razorpay Payment Page transaction record and onboarding fields. A screenshot alone is not final confirmation.

5

Manual Wafid step

AVC confirms the separate official Wafid cost where applicable, completes the official Wafid booking step and communicates the slip/status.

Payment confirmation

Razorpay receipt first, AVC reconciliation next.

After successful payment, Razorpay can send an automated receipt and redirect the customer to AVC's payment follow-up page. AVC checks the transaction in Razorpay before starting the booking.
