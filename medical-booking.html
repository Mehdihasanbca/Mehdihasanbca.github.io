Processing lifecycle

What happens after a verified payment when the AVC service is available.

1

PAID

Razorpay payment is server-verified and AVC creates the private medical case.

2

BOOKING IN PROGRESS

AVC operator enters the approved details on the official Wafid system.

3

OTP REQUIRED

If Wafid requires candidate verification, AVC contacts the candidate. AVC never bypasses official OTP controls.

4

SLIP AVAILABLE

AVC records the official Wafid slip/reference and, where uploaded, makes the slip available through secure case verification.

5

RESULT AVAILABLE

Medical result remains controlled by the official Wafid/accredited medical-center process.
