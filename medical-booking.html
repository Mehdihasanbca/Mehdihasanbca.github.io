Medical booking assistance with clear manual processing.

Manual Wafid step, structured AVC workflow.

AVC can manage the support case around payment, booking status and slip delivery, while the actual Wafid booking is completed on Wafid's official system.

How it works

AVC-assisted medical booking workflow.

1

Request

Contact AVC through the official route and ask for Wafid medical booking assistance. Do not send card PIN, CVV, UPI PIN or banking passwords.

2

Case & fee

AVC confirms the required candidate details, appointment route, third-party Wafid cost and AVC assistance/service charge before payment.

3

Payment

When secure Razorpay checkout is activated, payment will be tied to an AVC case reference and verified server-side.

4

Wafid booking

AVC operator completes the relevant Wafid booking step on the official Wafid system. Candidate OTP/verification may be required where Wafid asks for it.

5

Slip & status

Once the booking is confirmed, AVC records the medical slip/booking reference and communicates the next step to the candidate.

Fee transparency

Third-party cost and AVC service fee stay separate.

No fixed amount is published here because official Wafid charges and service arrangements may change. The candidate should see the current breakup before paying.
