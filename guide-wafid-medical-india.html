Candidate guide / medical stage

Wafid medical for GCC employment: know what is official, what to check, and what you are paying for.

Wafid is a Gulf Health Council program for medical examinations of people travelling to GCC states for work or residence. This guide explains the official flow and keeps AVC assistance clearly separate from Wafid's own charges.

Prepared by: Assignment Venue CenterReviewed by: Mehdi Hasan, Founder & Business HeadUpdated: 20 August 2026

Wafid is not owned or operated by AVC. Official Wafid rules, fees, appointment availability and medical-centre allocation can change; check wafid.com before acting.

Medical processing is one of the stages where candidates can easily confuse three different things: the official Wafid appointment, the medical examination performed by an accredited centre, and a private assistance service that helps a candidate enter details or coordinate the booking. Keeping those roles separate prevents fee confusion and reduces the chance of booking with incorrect passport data.

1. What Wafid is

Wafid describes itself as a program under the Gulf Health Council for individuals bound to work or reside in GCC countries. India is among the countries listed under the program. Through Wafid, candidates can book an appointment and later check medical examination results/status using the official website.

Wafid is commonly still referred to informally as GAMCA by candidates and agents, but the current official public service is branded Wafid. When someone sends you a “GAMCA link,” verify that the destination is the official Wafid domain or an explicitly identified assistance provider—not a look-alike payment page.

2. Book at the correct stage of the recruitment process

A medical should normally happen when the responsible recruitment process requires it. Booking too early can create unnecessary cost, wrong destination details or timing problems. Before booking, confirm the destination country, visa/employment context, passport details and whether the employer/Recruiting Agent has given any specific instruction.

Selection first, medical when instructedDo not assume that paying for a Wafid appointment proves you are selected for a job. Medical fitness is one requirement in a larger employment process; it is not a job guarantee.

3. The official Wafid booking flow

Wafid's FAQ directs applicants to the official website, then “Book an Appointment.” The candidate selects the appointment location country and city, the GCC country of travel, enters required personal/passport information and submits after checking the details.

The official booking page currently shows Standard and Premium appointment options. At the time this guide was reviewed, Wafid displayed US$10 for Standard and US$25 for Premium. Premium provides more control over medical centre/date selection. These are Wafid-displayed amounts and can change, so verify the live Wafid page rather than relying on this article for the current price.

Wafid may send an OTP during booking. The OTP is for your Wafid transaction. Do not share it with unrelated people or anyone who cannot explain why they are operating the booking on your behalf.

4. Candidate details that deserve a second check

A typing error in a casual enquiry is annoying; an error in a medical record can create a mismatch with passport and employment processing. Compare each entry directly with the passport.

Before submitting

  • First name and last name spelling exactly as required by the form/passport.
  • Date of birth.
  • Nationality.
  • Passport number and confirmation field.
  • Passport issue date, place and expiry date.
  • Gender and marital status where requested.
  • Visa type/travel purpose where requested.
  • Email and phone number you can actually access.
  • Destination GCC country.
  • Position applied for, if included and known.

Do not “correct” the passport name into a preferred spelling. The medical record must identify the same person as the passport used in the recruitment process.

5. Separate the official Wafid charge from AVC assistance

If a candidate uses AVC for Wafid booking assistance, AVC's published service price is ₹1,500 + ₹270 GST = ₹1,770. That amount is for AVC assistance only. The official Wafid/third-party amount is separate and should be shown separately.

This distinction is important for both transparency and refund discussions. A service provider cannot label a Wafid charge as its own fee, and a candidate should not assume that paying an AVC assistance fee automatically includes every official medical/Wafid charge unless the page explicitly says so.

AVC's web booking/payment route is designed to remain disabled if its secure server-side verification is not available. In that situation, use the official Wafid website or follow the responsible employer/Recruiting Agent instruction rather than sending money to a personal account.

6. Medical centre assignment and appointment

Under the Standard flow, Wafid explains that the medical centre is assigned based on the selected city. The Premium option may offer more control. Use the centre details shown on the official appointment/slip and confirm travel time before the appointment.

Take the documents specified by the current Wafid/medical-centre instruction. Requirements can change, so do not build a checklist from an old WhatsApp message. Arrive with the same passport used for the booking and keep a copy of the appointment slip/reference.

7. Checking medical status or results

Wafid provides an official medical-status search. The public search supports passport-number/nationality or GCC slip-number based lookup. Use the official page when checking a result instead of paying someone merely to “unlock” a report.

Keep your Wafid slip/reference with the recruitment file. If the employer or RA asks for the result, share it through the appropriate verified process. Medical information is sensitive personal data; avoid posting it in public groups.

8. Common booking problems and what to do

Name/passport mistake

Do not create multiple duplicate bookings blindly. Check the official Wafid FAQ/support route for the current correction procedure and notify the responsible recruitment party if the mismatch affects processing.

No appointment slot

Availability can vary by city/centre and appointment type. Recheck the official system or follow the authorized process; do not pay an unknown person who promises to “open a slot.”

Someone asks for an extra “fit guarantee” fee

No legitimate service can guarantee a medical fitness result in exchange for money. The examination outcome must come from the accredited medical process.

Payment made but no AVC case/reference

Use AVC's published payment reconciliation/contact route and provide transaction evidence. A screenshot alone is not treated as server-side payment verification in AVC's published payment-trust model.
