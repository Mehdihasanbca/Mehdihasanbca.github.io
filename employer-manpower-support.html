01 / SCOPE

What moves through this service.

Requirement should include

  • Job title and required quantity
  • Country / project / work location
  • Salary and benefits approved for communication
  • Experience, age or trade criteria when applicable
  • Interview method and target timeline
  • Responsible recruitment contact / route

AVC support

  • Requirement clarification
  • Candidate sourcing and profile organization
  • Shortlist support
  • Interview coordination
  • Candidate communication and attendance follow-up
  • Post-selection coordination with the responsible stakeholder

Operating boundary

Recruitment support and regulated processing remain distinct.

AVC's public role is recruitment support and manpower coordination. Regulated overseas recruitment activity remains with the concerned authorized employer / Recruiting Agent and applicable process.
