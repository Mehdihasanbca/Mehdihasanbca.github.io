Service 06

Employer manpower support

AVC supports employers and recruitment partners with manpower requirement intake, candidate sourcing, profile coordination, interview support and recruitment follow-up.

Focus: organized sourcing and coordination for clearly defined manpower requirements.

Support for manpower requirements

  • Requirement intake by job title and quantity
  • Eligibility, experience and location clarification
  • Candidate sourcing and profile organization
  • Shortlist and interview coordination support
  • Candidate communication and attendance follow-up
  • Post-selection coordination with the concerned recruitment stakeholder

Useful requirement details

  • Job title and number of workers
  • Project / country / work location
  • Salary and benefits approved for candidate communication
  • Experience, age or skill criteria where applicable
  • Interview method and timeline
  • Responsible employer / recruitment contact
Operating approach

Clear requirements make sourcing faster and more accurate.

1

Define

Requirement details are clarified.

2

Source

Relevant candidate profiles are collected.

3

Screen

Profiles are organized for stakeholder review.

4

Coordinate

Interview and next-step communication is supported.

AVC's public role is recruitment support and manpower coordination. Any regulated overseas recruitment activity remains subject to the concerned authorized recruitment stakeholders and applicable requirements.
