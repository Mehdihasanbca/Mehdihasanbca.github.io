1. Building Electrician & Industrial MEP Interview Standards

In Saudi Arabia, UAE, and Qatar, electrical hiring delegations evaluate candidates on both theoretical safety standards and hands-on circuit wiring. Street brokers often tell candidates "kuch nahi puchte, bas baithna hai"—this is false. Client testing engineers at AVC's 415V switchgear bays grade candidates on practical speed and wire dressing.

Top 5 Practical Wiring Tests:

  1. Forward / Reverse Three-Phase Motor Starter: Connecting power contactors, thermal overload relay (TOR), and auxiliary interlocking contacts.
  2. Star-Delta (Y-Δ) Control Circuit: Correct timer (on-delay) configuration preventing direct line short circuits during transition.
  3. Conduit Bending & Cable Tray Fitting: 90-degree stub bends, saddle bends, and clean 45-degree angle cable tray cutting with zero burrs.
  4. Distribution Board (DB) Dressing: Balancing 3-phase R-Y-B loads, MCB/RCCB wiring, and clean neutral/earth busbar lug termination.
  5. Insulation Resistance (Megger Test): Testing phase-to-phase and phase-to-ground insulation with a 500V or 1000V Megger meter. Minimum acceptable reading: > 1 Megaohm.

2. 6G TIG & ARC Pipe Welder Test Tips (Lincoln Bay Standards)

6G welding (pipe fixed at a 45-degree angle without turning) is the highest-paying mechanical trade in the Gulf (AED 2,200–3,200 + Overtime). Testing at AVC is conducted on calibrated Lincoln Electric machines under ASME Section IX standards.

Critical Test Failure Point: 80% of rejections in 6G tests happen in the Root Run due to lack of penetration (LOP) or concavity at the 6 o'clock position (overhead).
  • Root Pass (GTAW / TIG): Use 2.4mm ER70S-6 filler wire with pure Argon gas flow at 10–12 LPM. Keep root gap uniform at 2.5mm to 3.0mm. Keyhole technique must be continuous.
  • Hot Pass & Fill (SMAW / ARC): Use E7018 low-hydrogen electrodes baked at 350°C. Thoroughly de-slag every pass with an electric wire brush before laying the next run.
  • Capping Pass: Reinforcement height must not exceed 2mm to 3mm. Undercut greater than 0.5mm causes instant X-Ray radiographic failure.

3. HVAC & Central Chiller Technician Preparation

Facility management companies in Qatar and UAE test technicians on systematic refrigeration cycle troubleshooting rather than memorized definitions.

  • Compressor Terminal Resistance: Finding Common (C), Start (S), and Run (R) terminals using a digital multimeter. Formula: \( R_{CS} + R_{CR} = R_{SR} \).
  • Pressure-Temperature (P-T) Chart Interpretation: Evaluating superheat (evaporator efficiency) and subcooling (condenser efficiency) using R-410A and R-134a manifold gauges.
  • Low-Pressure / High-Pressure (LP/HP) Cut-Out Controls: Testing safety switch trips and differential pressure adjustments.

4. Heavy Trailer & Equipment Drivers: Practical Yard Test

Gulf transport employers (Saudi Arabia, Oman) require drivers to perform reverse alley-docking of 40-foot articulated trailers between cones.

  • Pre-Trip Vehicle Inspection: Checking air brake pressure (minimum 8 bar), tire tread depth, kingpin lock engagement, and dual air hoses (service & emergency lines).
  • 45-Degree & 90-Degree Reverse Docking: Use side mirrors only; leaning out of the window or touching boundary cones results in instant deduction.
  • GCC Road Sign Recognition: Mandatory knowledge of standard Arabic/English warning and speed limit signage.
