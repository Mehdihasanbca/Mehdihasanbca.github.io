Responsibility boundary

Support is not the same as overseas recruitment authorization.

AVC provides recruitment support and coordination. Final selection, recruitment approval, visa processing, emigration and overseas deployment are handled by the concerned employer and/or registered Recruiting Agent, subject to applicable law and verification.
