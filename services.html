03 / OPERATING BOUNDARY

Recruitment support and regulated overseas processing are separate responsibilities.

AVC support

  • Candidate sourcing and profile collection
  • Application and screening coordination
  • Profile/document readiness guidance
  • Interview communication and scheduling
  • Employer and recruitment-partner coordination

Authorized process

  • Final selection and recruitment approval
  • Visa and emigration formalities where applicable
  • Formal employment documentation
  • Travel and overseas deployment
  • Government/embassy procedures requiring authorization
NO GUARANTEE

Registration or support through AVC does not guarantee selection, visa approval or deployment.
