Service 04

Document guidance

AVC provides checklist-based document readiness guidance according to the actual vacancy, employer and recruitment process.

Principle: collect only relevant documents and use verified channels for sharing sensitive information.

Typical readiness support

  • Passport and identity document readiness where required
  • CV, experience and role-supporting documents
  • Education or skill documents relevant to the vacancy
  • Candidate photos or videos when a verified job specifically requires them
  • Checklist follow-up for missing or unclear items

Safety rules

  • Do not share sensitive documents with unknown accounts
  • Check why each document is being requested
  • Keep copies of important submissions and written communication
  • Do not alter or fabricate documents
  • Use official AVC channels and verified recruitment contacts
Document guidance is support for readiness and coordination. AVC does not issue passports, government approvals or official recruitment documents on behalf of authorities.
