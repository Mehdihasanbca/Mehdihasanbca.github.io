01 / SCOPE

What moves through this service.

Typical readiness

  • Passport / identity documents when required
  • CV and role-supporting experience documents
  • Relevant education or skill documents
  • Candidate photo or work video when specifically requested
  • Follow-up on missing or unclear items

Candidate safeguards

  • Share sensitive files only through known routes
  • Check why each document is required
  • Keep copies of important submissions
  • Do not alter or fabricate documents
  • Confirm unusual requests before sending
