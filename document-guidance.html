01 / DOCUMENT MATRIX

Separate common profile material from stage-specific sensitive documents.

This matrix is preparation guidance. The actual requirement should come from the verified vacancy or recruitment process.

CV / PROFILE

Current CV, role, experience summary and relevant work history.

Useful early for application and screening; keep facts consistent with genuine records.

IDENTITY

Passport or other identity details when the verified process specifically requires them.

Sensitive identity files should be shared only through known and applicable recruitment routes.

SKILL / TRADE

Role-relevant training, skill records, licences or trade evidence genuinely held.

Do not create certificates, licences or experience proof to fill a vacancy requirement.

EDUCATION

Education or qualification records when relevant to the vacancy or processing stage.

Unrelated records do not need to be attached simply because they are available.

PHOTO / VIDEO

Candidate photo, introduction video or work video only when the verified requirement asks for it.

Media should support the real role and should not expose unrelated private information.

LATER STAGE

Medical, visa, emigration or other processing records when that stage is actually applicable.

Do not treat later-stage requirements as universal application documents.

02 / SHARE CONTROL

Before sending a sensitive file, answer four questions.

01 / WHAT

What is requested?

Identify the exact document, page, image or video rather than sending an entire folder automatically.

02 / WHY

Why is it needed?

Understand whether it supports screening, identity verification, interview preparation or a later processing stage.

03 / WHO

Who requested it?

Check the AVC, employer or registered Recruiting Agent route as applicable before sharing sensitive information.

04 / WHERE

Where should it go?

Use the confirmed channel or application process instead of forwarding sensitive files to unknown numbers or links.

03 / PREPARATION STANDARD

Make genuine documents easy to review without modifying their meaning.

Good preparation

  • Use clear, readable scans or photos when a copy is required
  • Keep full page edges and important fields visible
  • Use simple filenames that identify the document type
  • Keep your own copy of important submitted records
  • Check dates, names and numbers for consistency before sharing

Do not do this

  • Edit dates, names, employer details or qualifications
  • Create fake experience letters or skill certificates
  • Hide material information to make a document appear different
  • Send every personal document before the process requires it
  • Share sensitive files through an unverified contact simply because the request sounds urgent
04 / STAGE LOGIC

Not every candidate reaches every document stage.

The order and exact documents vary by vacancy, employer, country and authorized processing route.

APPLICATION

Profile-first

Start with the factual candidate information and vacancy-specific items actually requested for screening.

SHORTLIST / INTERVIEW

Requirement-specific

Additional work evidence, documents or interview materials may be requested for the actual role.

SELECTED / PROCESSING

Authorized handoff

Later formalities are handled through the concerned employer and/or registered Recruiting Agent as applicable.

DOCUMENT SAFETY

AVC can guide readiness but does not issue passports, government approvals, visas or authority documents. Unusual document or payment requests should be verified before action.

05 / NEXT HANDOFF

Documents should support the recruitment stage—not drive it.

AVC provides checklist-based document readiness and coordination. Final document acceptance, formal recruitment processing, visa/emigration and deployment remain with the concerned employer, registered Recruiting Agent and applicable authorities.
